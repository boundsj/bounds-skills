Retains the catalog of filler, inflated vocabulary, vague attribution, mannered
prose, over-compression, and chat residue, moved to a reference. Adds an explicit
preservation contract for meaning, commitments, numbers, uncertainty, citations,
quotes, code, and required structure, plus audience and house-style matching. Removes
stable rule numbering, absolute punctuation bans, global application to all writing,
and explicit-only metadata.

Workflow design was informed by a private, generalized summary of one user's
engineering practices. No transcripts, private links, host addresses, or personal
project details are distributed. Integrations remain optional.

also retains Matt Pocock's MIT notice for consistency with its existing packages.
