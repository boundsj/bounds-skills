Retains fixing known issues before convening, one strong model per provider at
high reasoning effort, user-named members first, T3 capability discovery and
parallel delegation, read-only seats, reporting an unreachable provider instead of
substituting, acting on results as they arrive, non-blocking seats with unscored
late feedback, scores out of 10 per round, the PR description table with
`no score`, and the fight against scope creep. Adds explicit-only use, seats
counted by provider from the host's catalog rather than named models, a minimum
of two providers, fresh-context seats on pinned copies so fixes can proceed,
reported versus requested models, `bounds-review`'s brief and finding ledger,
plans and designs as subjects, and a bound of 3 rounds (up to 5 for a security,
data-loss, or concurrency change while a blocker is open) with disputes
returned to the user.
Removes the fixed three-provider requirement, example model names, and the loop
until every delegate is satisfied.
