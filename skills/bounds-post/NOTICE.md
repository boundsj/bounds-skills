Retains mining a fixed pile of material for fragments and a leading word,
grounding each concept before a paragraph relies on it, candidate openings that
imply different claims, paragraph tests and deliberate format choices, naming
gaps instead of filling them, ending when the argument is complete, and
specific, rhythmic sentences with periods instead of dashes. Adds user-named
sources only, one idea with its reader, proof, and honest limit, a claim check
against sources, a privacy pass with a listed change for every edit, and a
return that never publishes. Removes the interactive fragment and beat
sessions, append-as-you-go file handling and save-path prompts, the Diataxis
mode split and instruction rules aimed at docs, the required unslop load,
upstream repository conventions such as writing real paths, and explicit-only
metadata.
