recommended answers, agent-owned fact finding, user-owned decisions, and

questioning a shared unstated belief, a three-question round limit, a check-in
instead of a question cap, and an idea mode built on widely published
product-discovery practice (product risks, forces of progress, interviewing for
past behavior, assumption mapping, and pre-mortems). Removes required sub-agent
dispatch.
