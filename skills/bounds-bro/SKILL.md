# Bounds bro

Say it again the way one person tells another: short sentences, everyday words, no jargon or internal labels. Restate the previous answer unless the user points at other text.

Keep the conclusion or decision, every caveat that would change what the user does, and the next step. Drop process detail, hashes, paths, and repetition unless needed to act. Add no new claims. Do not soften a warning or turn "probably" into "will".

Usually no tools. This is only a restatement: do not resume implementation or take the next step.
